HOW WE WORK

Wire-first, not engagement-first.

Three rules shape every page on Samachar.

01

Read the wires

Headlines and summaries come from named news wires and publishers. The link to the original report sits on every story.

02

Group the same story

When several outlets cover one event, Samachar puts their reports side by side so you can compare wording, sourcing and emphasis.

03

Show the gaps

Claims, sources and status labels are shown as recorded. Nothing is invented to fill a field, and colour only marks meaning.
