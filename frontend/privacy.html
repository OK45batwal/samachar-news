PRIVACY POLICY & DATA INTEGRITY

Your Privacy Is Absolute

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Effective across all Samachar News Intelligence portals.

1. Core Privacy Manifesto

Samachar News is an autonomous, truth-first news verification network. We reject ad-supported surveillance business models. We do not sell your personal data, construct behavioral ad profiles, or deploy invasive third-party cross-site trackers.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address, display name, and securely hashed passwords (Argon2 / BCrypt).
  • Two-Factor Authentication Telemetry: Email addresses provided for 2-step verification (OTP) are used strictly for cryptographic session tokens.
  • Reading Preferences & Saved Bookmarks: Your bookmarked stories and customized channel filters are stored encrypted in your private session account.

3. Zero-Tracking Cookies & Analytics

We utilize strictly functional, HTTP-only authentication tokens (`access_token`) and local preferences (`samachar_theme`). We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies.

4. Security & Cryptographic Protection

All network communications are enforced over TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords undergo salt-hashing, and API endpoints are guarded with automated IP rate-limiting to prevent brute-force intrusion.

5. User Data Rights & Account Deletion

You retain 100% ownership over your intelligence archive. You may export or permanently delete your account, bookmarks, and fact-check history at any time from your Profile settings.

6. Contact Our Data Protection Team

For privacy questions, audits, or legal inquiries, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@samachar.news.
