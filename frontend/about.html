🏛️ ARCHITECTURAL MISSION

Restoring Absolute Factuality to Modern News.

Samachar is an autonomous truth-first news platform engineered to eliminate sensationalism and uncorroborated clickbait through automated multi-wire cross-referencing and claim deconstruction.

The 4-Stage Autonomous Truth Pipeline

STAGE 01

Continuous Multi-Wire Ingestion

Every 30 minutes, our distributed ingestion workers poll accredited wire feeds including Reuters, Associated Press, BBC News, Bloomberg, Nature Journal, and The Hindu, harvesting primary raw dispatches with zero human editorial bias.

STAGE 02

Semantic Clustering & Entity Triangulation

Incoming dispatches are matched against an active geopolitical knowledge graph. Related articles from independent publishers are grouped together to evaluate whether multiple accredited agencies independently verify the core events.

STAGE 03

Sensationalism & Adjective Penalty Engine

Headlines and summaries undergo NLP parsing to identify hyperbolic modifiers (e.g. "SHOCKING", "UNBELIEVABLE", "MIRACLE"), clickbait syntax, and uncredited assertions. Sensationalist phrasing incurs a direct quantitative penalty on the story's score.

STAGE 04

0–100% Truth Scorecard & Claim Deconstruction

Every published article is paired with an transparent Truth Scorecard breaking down key quantitative assertions, evidence citations, and wire agency corroboration counts before it reaches your feed.
