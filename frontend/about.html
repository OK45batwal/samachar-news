About Samachar

Real-time news intelligence powered by AI

Our Mission

Samachar delivers real-time news intelligence with AI-powered insights. We aggregate, analyze, and present news from thousands of sources worldwide, helping readers stay informed with clarity and context.

Our Technology

Built on a modern stack with real-time WebSocket streaming, machine learning-driven content classification, and an interactive geospatial map, Samachar brings you news as it breaks — with the intelligence to understand what matters.

Key Features

  • Live Updates — Real-time WebSocket-powered news feed
  • AI Insights — Machine learning summaries and analysis
  • World Map — Geospatial news visualization
  • Trending Topics — Algorithmically detected trending stories
  • Reading History — Local storage-based personal history
  • Bookmarks — Save articles for later
