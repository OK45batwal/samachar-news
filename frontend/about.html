About Samachar

Samachar was engineered with a single uncompromising mission: to restore absolute factual clarity to online journalism through autonomous multi-source cross-corroboration, claim extraction, and sensationalism elimination.

Our 4-Stage Truth Verification Pipeline

1. Multi-Feed Parallel Ingestion

We continuously stream raw RSS and structured feeds from 60+ accredited international, national, and scientific news wires every 30 minutes.

2. Entity Clustering & Corroboration

Natural language processors identify shared geopolitical entities, numerical metrics, and dates across concurrent reports from different publishers.

3. Sensationalism & Clickbait Indexing

Headlines and lead paragraphs are audited for emotional hyperbole, loaded adjectives, and uncredited speculation.

4. Algorithmic Truth Scorecard

Every story receives an objective Truth Score (0–100%) and a transparent claim-by-claim evidence breakdown before reaching the reader.
